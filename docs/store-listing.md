## Title
**LinkedIn Outreach AI & Job-Search Copilot**

*Smart outreach and job application copilot for LinkedIn. Tailored cover notes, job match analysis, and CRM. You click Apply.*

## Detailed Description

Stop sending generic connection notes and cookie-cutter job applications. **LinkedIn Outreach AI** is your privacy-first, human-in-the-loop copilot that transforms how you discover jobs, analyze requirements, and connect with hiring teams on LinkedIn.

**"You click Apply. We prepare everything."**

### ⚡ Free Mode: Fully Functional Out of the Box
No API key? No problem. Free Mode operates 100% locally on your machine with deterministic templates, keyword extraction, and weighted fit scoring. Your data never leaves your device.

### 🧠 AI Mode: Claude-Powered Precision
Add your personal Anthropic API key to unlock deep analysis powered by Claude 3.5. Parse resumes, synthesize multi-layer personalization signals, and craft nuanced messages tailored to specific posts and company news.

---

### Key Features

#### 1. 🔍 Job-Search Copilot & Smart Search Suggestions
- Set your Career Profile (target roles, key skills, locations, work preferences).
- Generate 3–5 targeted LinkedIn job search links with automatic filter presets.
- Rank all rendered jobs on any search page with transparent **Strong / Good / Stretch** fit indicators and clear, 1-line explanations (no fabricated percentages).

#### 2. 📋 Comprehensive Application Kit
On any LinkedIn job posting, generate an end-to-end Application Kit in seconds:
- **Match Analysis:** Honest breakdown of matched strengths and real qualification gaps.
- **Tailored Resume Highlights:** 3–4 bullet points strictly derived from your actual experience.
- **Keyword Checklist:** Interactive checklist showing which required keywords are in your resume vs. missing.
- **Tailored Cover Note:** Concise, persuasive cover note under 1,200 characters ready to paste into applications.
- **Screening Question Answers:** Pre-drafted responses to common application questions with explicit placeholders for facts not on your resume.
- **Alumni & Recruiter Finder:** Instant 1-click links to find recruiters, hiring managers, and university alumni at the hiring company.

#### 3. 🎯 3-Layer Outreach Intelligence
When viewing any LinkedIn profile, discover high-signal conversation starters:
- Shared education and background overlap
- Recent posts and thought leadership
- Relevant achievements and trajectory
- 4 message types: Networking, Job Inquiry, Referral Request, Collaboration
- 3 tones: Formal, Neutral, Casual
- Bounded lengths: 300-char connection note, 500-char DM, 900-char message

#### 4. 🗂️ Built-in Application Tracker & CRM
- Save jobs directly from search results or job pages.
- Track status: Saved, Applied, Interviewing, Offered, Rejected.
- Automated 7-day follow-up reminder alerts.
- Interactive Apply Assist checklist (resume tailored, cover note copied, contact saved, applied).
- One-click client-side CSV export for your spreadsheets.
- Built-in outreach note counter with soft reminders to encourage quality over spam.

---

### Strict Privacy & LinkedIn Compliance
- **No automation:** We NEVER click Apply, Connect, Send, or submit forms on LinkedIn. You maintain 100% control over every action.
- **No background scraping:** We only inspect the page currently active in your browser.
- **Zero data collection:** We operate no backend servers, analytics trackers, or user databases.

---

### Permissions Justification
- `activeTab`: To inspect the visible LinkedIn profile or job posting when you click the extension.
- `storage`: To save your settings, career profile, application tracker, and recent contacts locally in Chrome.
- `tabs`: To open targeted search results and navigate to saved job links in new tabs.
- `scripting`: To read rendered page elements defensively without breaking if LinkedIn updates its DOM.
